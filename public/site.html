What is Shalya Tantra?

Shalya Tantra is the ancient Ayurvedic surgical discipline described in the Sushruta Samhita. It is considered the world's first surgical science, covering surgical procedures, instruments, and treatment protocols that predate modern surgery by millennia. Sushruta, known as the father of surgery, described over 300 surgical procedures and 120 surgical instruments.

What is the Sushruta Copilot?

The Sushruta Copilot is QuaasX 108's AI surgical co-pilot system that provides real-time AI guidance during surgical procedures. It integrates knowledge from the Sushruta Samhita and other classical Ayurvedic texts (Charaka Samhita, Ashtanga Hridaya, Ashtanga Sangraha, and 19+ more texts) with modern evidence-based surgical protocols, creating a hybrid intelligence system for surgical decision support.
